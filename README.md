# Capsule

**Say it now. Read it later.**

Capsule is a personal time capsule for your phone. Write a note to your future
self, add photos or files, choose the day it opens, and seal it. Until that day
comes, the capsule stays locked — no preview, no peeking, no early exit. When the
open day arrives, Capsule brings it back to you.

---

## How it works

### 1. Write a capsule

Tap **New capsule** and add:

- **A title** — what you'll see on the open day.
- **A note** — plain text with simple formatting. Say whatever future you needs to hear.
- **Photos and files** *(optional)* — attach the moment, not just the words.
- **An open date** — tomorrow, next birthday, five years out. Your choice.

You can save it as a **draft** and come back later. Nothing is locked until you seal it.

### 2. Seal it

Sealing is final. Once a capsule is sealed:

- You can't read it, edit it, or export it until the open day.
- Not even you can open it early.
- You can still delete it — but you'll never get to read what was inside.

Turn on **biometric seal** in your profile if you want Face ID / fingerprint
required to seal and to open.

### 3. Wait

Sealed capsules live on your home screen with a countdown to their open day.
Capsule handles the rest.

### 4. Open day

When a capsule's day arrives:

- You get a notification — even if the app is closed.
- The capsule unlocks. Read the note, look back at the photos.
- It moves to your **opened** list, where it stays for good.

---

## Setting up

Open your **profile** to adjust:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **PIN** | Required to open the app. Set during first run. |
| **Biometric seal** | Use Face ID / fingerprint to seal and open capsules. |
| **Notifications** | Get alerted the moment a capsule is ready. Off until you allow it. |
| **Open time** | The time of day every capsule opens (default 8:00 AM). Changing it shifts every capsule, sealed ones included. |
| **Birthday** | Lets Capsule suggest your next birthday as an open date. |
| **Name & photo** | Shown on your profile. |
| **Theme** | Light or dark. |

---

## Your data stays with you

- Everything is stored **only on this device**. There is no account, no cloud, no sync.
- Capsule never sends your notes or photos anywhere.
- If you delete the app or wipe your capsules, they're gone — there's no backup to restore from.

### Deleting everything

**Delete all capsules** in your profile starts a **7-day grace period**. During
that week you can cancel and keep everything. After seven days, every capsule is
permanently erased.

---

## Good to know

- **A sealed capsule cannot be recovered early.** If it's urgent, don't seal it —
  keep it as a draft.
- **Notifications need OS permission.** If open days pass silently, check that
  Capsule is allowed to send notifications in your system settings.
- **Changing the open time moves everything.** It's a single global setting, not
  per capsule.
- Losing your device means losing your capsules. Treat them as one-of-a-kind.
